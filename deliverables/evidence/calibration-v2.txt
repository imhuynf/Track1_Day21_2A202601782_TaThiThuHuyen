Stop decision
-------------
Groundedness v2 is within roughly 3 percentage points of the 93% overall human-human
agreement reference, so another prompt round risks trading TPR for TNR. Follow-up v2
reaches 100% on the same calibration rows used to select its examples; this may be
overfit and is not a substitute for one-time validation on a fresh held-out test set.
